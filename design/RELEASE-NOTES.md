# What's new — a fresh look for POS Desktop

*For shop owners and counter staff. Version number to be filled in at release.*

Everything works the way it did — the same sales, stock, customers and money. It just looks
cleaner, is easier to read, and a few things that were confusing now say what they mean.

## Pick your look
- **Light, dark, or follow the computer.** Switch at the top right of any screen, or in
  **Settings → Appearance**. This is per computer.
- **Your shop's colour.** The owner can choose the accent colour (buttons, links, highlights) in
  **Settings → Appearance → Accent colour**. Every counter gets it at the next sign-in.
- **Navy or light sidebar**, also in **Settings → Appearance** (owner).

## The till
- Products show their initials on a colour instead of the same 📦 for everything.
- Out-of-stock products are greyed out and say **Out of stock** — no more finding out after tapping.
- Bigger, clearer **Pay** button; the payment window shows **Change to give** in large type,
  quick cash buttons (Exact, 500, 1,000 …) and a warning when the cash is short.
- **Barcode scanners are safer**: the cursor always goes back to the scan box, and a scan can no
  longer press a button by accident (a second item, a new bill, or finishing a sale).
- "Item added" messages now appear at the top, so they never cover the Pay button.

## Lists and reports
- Every list (Bills, Products, Customers, Expenses, Supplies …) has the same layout: search, one
  **Filter** button, sortable columns, and you can hide columns or make rows tighter.
- Totals at the top of each screen are clickable where it helps (e.g. Receipts → today's sales).
- Reports: clearer charts, **Day / Week / Month** for sales over time, and every chart can be shown as
  a table or saved as CSV.
- Customer ledger: a summary with a **Bought vs paid** chart and their top products.

## Stock
- One rule for stock warnings everywhere: products that don't keep a stock count say **Not tracked**
  (they sell normally and never warn), **0** is **Out of stock**, and at or below the product's alert
  level is **Low**. The counts on Products and Inventory now agree.

## Closing the day
- A tidier closing screen: the period at the top, headline figures, and cash / card / online boxes
  where you type what you counted — the difference updates as you type.
- Locking a closing asks once more before it's final.

## Settings, sign in and small fixes
- Settings is organised into cards (You, Shop, Receipts and printing, Selling rules, Appearance, App).
- Counter staff now see **their own** name and sign-in ID in Settings (it used to show placeholder
  admin details).
- The staff page uses your shop's own name for staff (e.g. "Counter Users").
- The sign-in screen has an **Owner / Staff** switch instead of two different pages.
- If you open a page your account isn't allowed to see, it now tells you so (before, it silently
  went back to the home screen).
- If a page can't load (no internet, server busy) it says so with a **Try again** button instead of
  showing zeros.
- Money is written the same way everywhere, with your shop's currency and at most 2 decimals.

## Good to know
- Fonts now come with the app, so it looks the same with or without internet.
- Nothing about your data, prices, balances or permissions has changed.
